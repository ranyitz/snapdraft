01The problem

A one-line edit shouldn't require a chatbot.

Most small rewrites start in Slack, Gmail, WhatsApp, or another text field. Moving them into a chatbot adds copying and often breaks formatting. It also leaves drafts scattered across tools.

  1. 01

    A small edit becomes a detour

    Opening another app to fix one sentence interrupts the work already in front of you. Snapdraft rewrites the selected text where it is.

  2. 02

    You repeat the same instructions

    "Make this clearer" and "turn this into an email" can be saved as rewrite modes instead of typed into a chat each time.

  3. 03

    Copy and paste breaks formatting

    Moving text through a chat tool can strip bullets, links, emphasis, and code. Snapdraft keeps that structure intact.

  4. 04

    Chat history is not draft history

    Snapdraft saves drafts and in-place rewrites locally, including the original text and its replacement.

03Shortcut behavior

What happens when you press the shortcut.

Snapdraft checks whether text is selected and whether you can edit it.

Nothing selected

Start a fresh draft

  1. ⌘ ⇧ Space
  2. Snapdraft opens at your saved screen position.
  3. Type or paste text, then choose a rewrite mode.
  4. Copy the result.
Editable text selected

Rewrite in place

  1. Select text in an editable field.
  2. ⌘ ⇧ Space
  3. The result replaces your selection.
Read-only text selected

Open and rewrite

  1. Select text in a page, message, or document.
  2. ⌘ ⇧ Space
  3. Snapdraft opens with the selection and starts rewriting.
  4. Review the result, then copy it where you need it.

05Privacy

Your drafts stay local by default.

With the local model, Snapdraft rewrites and transcribes on your Mac. Draft history stays there too. If you connect ChatGPT, Snapdraft sends the text you choose to rewrite to OpenAI.

  • Rewriting runs locally through llama.cpp.
  • Transcription runs locally through whisper.cpp.
  • Draft history is stored on your Mac.
  • Models download on first use and are cached locally.
  • ChatGPT rewrites send only the selected draft through your account. Snapdraft does not send your history or clipboard.

06Install

Install Snapdraft.

Download latest DMG All releases
  1. Download the latest Snapdraft.dmg.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Snapdraft into Applications.
  3. Launch Snapdraft from Applications.
  4. Press ⌘+⇧+Space to open the drafting overlay.

Requires macOS 13.3 or later and Apple silicon. Local rewriting runs best with 16 GB of memory or more. On an 8 GB Mac, use your ChatGPT account for faster rewrites.

If installation fails, open an issue on GitHub.
